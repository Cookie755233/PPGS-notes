## Departing Procedures
==Operations → Traffic → Ground → Tower → Approach(NORCAL) >>==

### Departing Mather
**COM2 on 133.3 — EVA Operations**

Pilot: EVA Operations, EVASTAR 805, block out 35, full tank

EVA Operations: EVASTAR 805, block out 35, full tank, have a nice flight

*After releasing brakes and taxiing*

Pilot: EVA Traffic, EVASTAR 805, taxiing out of T-shed via T1

*Hold short Taxiway A at T1*

*Pilot switches from COM2 to COM1 with 121.85 (Mather Ground) active*

### Taxi Clearance
**COM1 on 121.85 (Mather Ground)**

Pilot: Mather Ground, EVASTAR 805, hold short taxiway A at T1, ready to taxi with Lima, ==request local area number==

Mather Ground: EVASTAR 805, taxi Runway 22R via A B, squawk 0365

Pilot: Taxi Runway 22R via A B, squawk 0365, EVASTAR 805

*Squawked 0365* 

*Pilot taxis to the runup area via A B, completes the runup and before takeoff check checklist*

*proceeds to hold short Runway 22R at taxiway B.*

### Takeoff Clearance
**COM1 on 120.65 — Mather Tower**

Pilot: Mather Tower, EVASTAR 805, hold short Runway 22R at B, ready for departure

Mather Tower: EVASTAR 805, Runway 22R cleared for takeoff

Pilot: Runway 22R cleared for takeoff, EVASTAR 805

*Pilot takes off*

### Transfer to NorCal
Mather Tower: EVASTAR 805, contact NorCal Departure 127.4

Pilot: Contact NorCal Departure 127.4, EVASTAR 805

*Pilot switches COM1 Active to 127.4 (NorCal Departure)*


## Flight Following with Norcal
### Initial Contact 
**COM1 on 127.4 (NorCal Departure)**

Pilot: NorCal Departure, EVASTAR 805, 1500 climbing 3500, heading to Franklin for Pattern Work

NorCal: EVASTAR 805, radar contact, Sacramento altimeter settings 29.95

Pilot: 29.95, EVASTAR 805

*Pilot sets altimeter settings to 29.95 inHg*

### Enroute
*Pilot listens to SAC ATIS on 125.5, completes approach briefing and descent/approach check checklist*

![](/images/enroutecomm.png "")

*Pilot decides to overfly midfield at 1500 ft, make right teardrop for left downwind 27.*

### Frequency Change
**Approximately 10 miles from Franklin**

NorCal: EVASTAR 805, cancel flight following, squawk VFR, frequency change approved

Pilot: Squawk VFR, frequency change approved, EVASTAR 805

*Pilot squawks 1200, changes COM1 Active to 122.9 (Franklin CTAF)*


## CTAF on Franklin
### Entry to First Landing
**COM1 on 122.9 (Franklin CTAF)**

Pilot: **Franklin Traffic**, Diamond 875BR, *10/5/3 miles* {{< tip "report on 3/5/7 miles" >}}NorthEast, going to overfly midfield 1500, make right teardrop for left downwind 27, **Franklin** {{< tip "Must call  twice" >}}

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, **overflying midfield** {{< tip "Enter an airport's traffic pattern" >}} 1500, making right teardrop for left downwind 27, Franklin

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, descending 1000, making right teardrop for left downwind 27, Franklin

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, *joining left downwind* 27 at a 45, Franklin

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, left *downwind* 27, touch and go, Franklin

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, turning left *base* 27, touch and go, Franklin

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, turning *final* 27, touch and go, Franklin

### Traffic Pattern at Franklin
Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, *upwind* 27, left closed traffic, Franklin

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, turning left *crosswind* 27, left closed traffic, Franklin

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, turning left *downwind* 27, touch and go, Franklin

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, turning left *base* 27, touch and go, Franklin

Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, turning *final* 27, touch and go, Franklin

### Departing Franklin (Straight Out Departure)
Pilot: Franklin Traffic, Diamond 875BR, upwind 27, making straight out departure, Franklin

*Pilot departs Franklin, flies back to Mather without flight following*

*Pilot listens to Mather ATIS (118.325), receives information Kilo, and completes descent/approach check checklist*


## Going back to Mather
### Initial Contact
**COM1 on 120.65 (Mather Tower)**

![](/images/goingbackmather.png "")

*Approximately 10 miles SouthWest of Mather*

Pilot: Mather Tower, EVASTAR805, 10 miles SouthWest, inbound to land with Kilo

Mather Tower: EVASTAR805, Join left downwind for Runway 22R

*Tower has asked Pilot to join 22L’s left traffic pattern, then transition to 22R from there*

Pilot: Join left downwind for Runway 22R, EVASTAR805

*Pilot joins the traffic pattern*

Mather Tower: EVASTAR 805, cleared to land Runway 22R

Pilot: Cleared to land Runway 22R, EVASTAR 805

*Pilot lands*

Mather Tower: EVASTAR 805, right turn taxiway D, contact ground 121.85

Pilot: Right turn taxiway D, contact ground 121.85, EVASTAR805

*Pilot changes COM1 active to 121.85 (Mather Ground)*

### Taxi Clearance
**COM1 on 121.85 (Mather Ground)**

Pilot: Mather Ground, EVASTAR 805, clear of Runway 22R at D, request taxi to park

Mather Ground: EVASTAR 805, taxi to park via A

Pilot: Taxi to park via A, EVASTAR 805

*Pilot taxis via A, then turns left on T3 to park*

*Pilot switches from COM1 to COM2 with 123.3 (EVA Operations) active*

### Taxiing to Park
**COM1 on 123.3 (EVA Operations)**

Pilot: EVA Traffic, EVASTAR 805, taxiing to park via T3

*Pilot parks, shuts off engine, and prepares to block in*

### Block In
*Time 1217 PM; Fuel 24.8 Gal; Maintenance required No (status green)*

Pilot: EVA Operations, EVASTAR 805, block in 17, fuel 25 gallons, status green

Eva Operations: EVASTAR 805, block in 17, fuel 25 gallons, status green, welcome back
